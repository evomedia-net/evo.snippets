confetti-burst

Paper falls slowly and flutters, because it is nearly all surface and almost no mass. This models that — drag, tumble, sway — rather than throwing dots on a ballistic arc.

Fire

Sound

Off. The renderer has no audio of its own — sound is the caller’s job, because browsers refuse to play it until the page has been interacted with.

Any position

Numbered like a numpad, so 1 3 7 9 are the four screen corners. Each carries its own aim, because a corner cannon firing straight up throws half its pieces off-screen.

Halves land between neighbours, so 7, 7.5, 8, 8.5, 9 is five evenly spaced across the top.

Using it

<script src="confetti.js"></script>
<script>
  confettiBurst();                        // straight up from the middle
  confettiBurst({ origin: 7 });           // from the top-left corner
  confettiBurst({ origin: 3, direction: 315, count: 200 });
</script>
